# Sources and External References

This document collects the primary sources behind the `eda-journal-writing` skill.

---

## Writing Philosophy

These sources are the basis of [writing-guide.md](writing-guide.md). They are largely venue-agnostic; the writing principles transfer cleanly across ML, EDA, systems, and other communities.

- G. D. Gopen and J. A. Swan, "The Science of Scientific Writing," *American Scientist*, 1990. <https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~swanson/papers/science-of-writing.pdf>
- Neel Nanda, "Highly Opinionated Advice on How to Write ML Papers," 2022. <https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/eJGptPbbFPZGLpjsp/highly-opinionated-advice-on-how-to-write-ml-papers>
- Sebastian Farquhar, "How to Write ML Papers," 2024. <https://sebastianfarquhar.com/on-research/2024/11/04/how_to_write_ml_papers/>
- Zachary Lipton, "Heuristics for Scientific Writing (a Machine Learning Perspective)," 2018. <https://www.approximatelycorrect.com/2018/01/29/heuristics-technical-scientific-writing-machine-learning-perspective/>
- Ethan Perez, "Easy Paper Writing Tips," 2021. <https://ethanperez.net/easy-paper-writing-tips/>
- Jacob Steinhardt, "Writing Tips" (Bounded Regret). <https://bounded-regret.ghost.io/>
- Andrej Karpathy — single-contribution focus, paper structure (various lectures).

## IEEE Author Resources

- **IEEE Author Center (Journals)** — submission policies, formatting, peer review process. <https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/>
- **IEEE Manuscript Templates** — official LaTeX templates and downloads. <https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html>
- **IEEE Reference Guide** — IEEE citation style reference. <https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/your-role-in-article-production/ieee-editorial-style-manual/>
- **IEEE Graphics Checker** — automated checker for figure quality. <https://graphicsqc.ieee.org/>
- **IEEE PDF eXpress** — PDF compliance checker for IEEE submissions. <https://ieee-pdf-express.org/>
